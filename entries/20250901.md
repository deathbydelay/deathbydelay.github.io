# September 2025

## A Wild Lump has appeared

In the early part of the month, I noticed something new. It was a lump on my neck near where the thing on my face was. At first the new thing was just a tiny lump, so I checked it each day for any changes. One thing that I discovered was if I pushed on it I would get a bit light headed. So obviously it was a problem and after a week of it growing in size and becoming more firm, I alerted my wife. We made an appointment with our primary who examined the old thing, the new thing, and other things deemed as problem areas and sent off referrals for us to see specialists.
