## The little hole that could

Over the course of the month the hole was closed, all the way until it wasn’t. The buccal flap had failed and my ENT felt terrible, but I didn't want him to feel bad because I was just on the train and checking out the sites as we passed stops along the way until the end of radiation. I was kept on antibiotics for a long time in the early part of the month to give the little hole time to close, but alas it did not. It simply wanted to be a hole, until one day when it seemed closed. Then another day it seemed closed. Then the third day it seemed closed. Despite feeling like the hole was closed it was not, plus there is a crevice where the hole is. Finally, we admitted defeat and this little hole will just close on its own, but to give the hole a fighting chance with radiation starting very soon we settled on having a plate created that would sit over the hole similar to how dentures would fit.

We also had to go back to the radiation office to make sure the mask still fit and get a new time slot for daily radiation.
