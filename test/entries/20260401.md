# April 2026

## Sunburn and Weight Loss

My face has been burning since about the start of week 2, which was halfway through March, but it was barely burned then. Now it’s burned so much that I’ve started putting lotion on to prevent it from getting too dry. The radiation techs told me that I could put lotion on before the appointment as long as it wasn’t shiny when it was appointment time. So we started putting it on in the morning before I left for work to keep my face from peeling.

My appetite has basically tanked, but that was expected by the radiation team because when I mentioned it to them they were not surprised at all. In fact, one of the techs told me that other radiation labs account for this and will install a feeding tube before radiation treatments even start. I’m glad that I didn’t have to get a feeding tube because I watched my wife go through that and it was not something I wanted to repeat for either of us. In addition to loss of appetite causing weight loss, I’ve also lost my ability to taste the food that I eat. It’s very discouraging to eat food that you know has a certain taste, but now tastes like nothing. The saliva that I produce now also has become viscous, and boy does it like to hang around. It is thick enough that it tends to make swallowing difficult.

It turns out that the hole between my sinuses and mouth never really did close. I found this out at work in the second to last week of radiation. I was moving some things around at work and it was dusty. I needed to sneeze but there were a lot of people around and my hands were dirty so I didn't want to touch my face or clothes, so I did the next instinctive thing and sneezed in my sinuses. It was all a total reflex and all those thoughts happened in the span of milliseconds, but afterwards i went to my office to get a drink of water and it was immediately apparent that the hole was back because air was moving through it while i was drinking. Not too happy about that, but we’ll take care of it after radiation treatments are over.

I struggled finding something that was appealing to eat. I tried to just eat what everyone was eating if it was a meal at home, but when you can’t taste anything you rely on texture and temperature. I’ve already got an issue with some food textures, so now I’m even more limited as to what works. Scrambled eggs work because there is enough of a difference between chunks that it doesn’t feel like one homogenous mass of goop. Chicken noodle soup works because it's a broth that has nutrients and the noodles are soft enough that I can chew them a bit and basically drink the broth. I tried peanut butter and jelly sandwiches, and it was ok, but since my mouth is already dry it made eating the bread a task. Regardless of what I was eating, this wasn’t the time to eat healthy but the time to just get something to prevent more weight loss. Basically preventing the feeding tube from becoming a reality.

The burn on my face has become really intense. The skin is dry and is very pink, nearly red. The radiation doctor prescribed Silver Sulfadiazine to counteract the skin burning as well. We’ve been putting it on in the morning and at night, then in the afternoon following radiation we just use the lotion we were using before swapping to the Silver Sulfadiazine. On the side of my face where the BCC was, the radiation has caused the scar there to fissure simply because it’s getting radiated the most. Also under my chin is scarred and scabbed a bit as well because again the radiation is focused in that area.

I did finish radiation near the middle of the month, and since then some things have returned to normal. I don’t have to do that baking soda rinse any longer since I never had any sores open up inside my mouth. I’m still wearing the obturator, although I sometimes forget to put it in before I leave home. The Silver Sulfadiazine is working well because despite going through all that radiation my skin looks as if it’s healing a bit towards the end. We saw my ENT the other day and he remarked that he wouldn’t recommend anyone go through radiation to improve the look of a scar, but was amazed at how good it looked compared to when we saw him prior to radiation. That’s something that I just don’t see because I see my face all the time, and to me it mostly just looks the same as the last time I saw it, but other people notice the differences. Same thing about how people see your kid after a while and make comments about how big they’ve gotten.

We met with the dentist, and after he examined the area where the hole is he recommended us to an Oral Surgeon. On the road after getting the referral we called and scheduled an appointment for next month. Also next month we have to go back to radiation to be checked out after being discharged. We also have to go back to the ENT to address concerns he may have and just generally follow up since they have been the main force driving all this treatment. We’re hoping that the hole between my sinuses and mouth will just heal on its own after we get the piece of tooth out. I’m actually getting used to wearing this obturator now and the only time I don't have it in is when I'm sleeping. Maybe by June I’ll have been fitted for dentures so I can get some teeth back so I can chew on both sides of my mouth…
