## New Medicine and More Sinus Surgery

I’m as high as a kite, and we arrived to have this piece of tooth extracted. The dentist walks in, greets us and is holding two pieces of paper. She shows us the first one and it’s the x-ray they took last time and the piece of tooth is visible, but then she shows us the other paper and it’s an x-ray they took before they pulled the teeth and the piece of tooth was present in that one as well. So this means that it’s not a piece of tooth that was left after they extracted teeth, it’s just some random bone fragment that was there all along. So all this time I’ve been upset at them for something they didn’t do. I expressed that to the dentist, but nobody was upset about anything and everyone was just focused on me getting the care I need. I had a good laugh about that, probably because I was loaded, but at least now we can focus on trying to get this hole closed.

The dentist referred us to an oral surgeon who had previously denied wanting to see us since they wanted the original dentist who left the piece of tooth to retrieve it, but now that those circumstances have changed they’ve agreed to see us. When we met with them, they discussed how surgery isn’t really viable right now. It would be a lot of work to go in and find a 2mm piece of bone fragment, and considering it’s been there for a while it’s not posing any immediate danger. Concerning the oroantral fistula, they want to try putting me on pentoxifylline and vitamin e. Those together should promote the tissue in the area to regrow and close the hole. They also talked about how this might not be repairable and I could have this hole forever. I asked if dentures could be made to seal the hole while I’m awake because it’s quite difficult to manage. They agreed that dentures would help with keeping the hole covered while also replacing some lost teeth, so that process should start soon. They sent me for a CT w/ contrast and will also restart the hyperbaric treatment discussion with my insurance company.

I also had a follow up with my ENT the same day as the CT w/ contrast, and he looked at the results that the oral surgeon will also see. He noticed some inflammation in the right sinus and wants to do a procedure to take care of that, and that procedure happens next month.
